The True Opportunity Cost Equation

Pure Economic Rationality
Total Route Economic Cost:
C_total = P_flight + C_ground + (T_ground × V_hour)
Net Traveler Savings:
Savings = C_home - C_alternate
Why this changes flight discovery forever:

If a traveler values their free time at $20/hour, a 2-hour rail ride costs them $40 in time value. If the flight saves $400, their net economic benefit is still $360.

Only when an individual's time value exceeds $320/hr does driving/taking the train to Orlando become economically irrational.

The Multimodal Moat

01. The Airline Ad Conundrum

Major airlines spend billions in sponsored listings on Google Flights. Promoting a $25 rail ride to a competing carrier's hub directly cannibalizes regional feeder flights.

02. GDS Architecture Constraints

Legacy Global Distribution Systems (Sabre, Amadeus) are strictly indexed by IATA 3-letter codes. They do not maintain overland ground transit speed or station adjacency matrixes.

03. Fragmented Transit Integrations

Connecting Brightline, Amtrak, European high-speed rail (DB, SNCF, SBB) requires specialized multimodal routing logic and realistic transfer buffer modeling.

04. Traveler Alignment

FlightDeck is 100% traveler-first. We calculate the exact opportunity cost to the traveler's wallet and time, creating immense organic user loyalty and viral sharing.
